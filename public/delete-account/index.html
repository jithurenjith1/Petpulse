Delete your account

This page explains how to have your Wagmiya account and the data linked to it deleted. You do not need to install the app to use this page.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — inside the app. Open Wagmiya, go to the Wagmiya tab, then Settings, then “Delete my account”. The request is recorded immediately.

Option 2 — from this page. Send us a message from the phone number or email address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We reply to confirm we have the right account before deleting anything.

Message on WhatsApp Email us

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record.
  • Your profile: name, phone number, email address and delivery addresses.
  • Your pet profiles, including photos, breed, age, weight, microchip number, vaccination records, certificates and medical reports you uploaded.
  • Your orders and bookings, and the cart.
  • Your community posts, comments and lost-pet or found-pet alerts that you created.
  • Your care-plan status and any saved preferences.

What may be kept, and why

  • Reviews you wrote. These stay attached to the dealer or service you reviewed, because removing them would change other customers’ records. They are shown without your account details.
  • Reports you filed about a listing or a user, where a safety or legal matter is still open.
  • Order and payment records that a dealer or the tax authorities are required to keep for their own statutory period. These are held by the dealer, not by us.
  • Aggregate counts that contain no personal information.

How long it takes

Deletion is completed within 30 days of us confirming the request. You will get a confirmation message when it is done. Anything kept under the section above is retained only for as long as it is needed for that purpose.

Before you delete. If you have an active order or booking, please wait until it is delivered or completed. Deleting the account does not cancel an order that is already with a dealer.
